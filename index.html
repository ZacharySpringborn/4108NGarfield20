The full picture

What buyers are weighing

Your home has advantages most of the competition can't match. Right now they aren't closing the price gap in buyers' minds.

Working in your favor

  • Lot rent well below most area parks, which lowers a buyer's monthly cost every month they live there
  • One of the best lots I've seen for a manufactured home: 8–10 mature trees and a large fenced yard, ideal for a retiree with a pet
  • Two storage sheds and a carport
  • New roof and updated flooring
  • Built in 1983, newer than most recent sales (median 1977)

Holding buyers back

  • The asking price is more than double the typical 55+ sale and $19,000 above the next-highest home for sale
  • Cosmetic work: most buyers will want new carpet and paint before moving in
  • Original windows, though the interior storm windows help with insulation
  • Limited photos, since the home couldn't be fully cleared before listing
  • Cash or chattel-loan buyers only, a budget-conscious group that compares the purchase price home to home

Is it too soon to worry? Thirty days isn't long in this segment. The 2026 sales above took a median of 106 days. But a home that's on track usually gets some showings in its first month. With none so far, waiting is unlikely to change the result by itself.

Next steps

Options to talk through

These can be combined. I'll come to our call with specific recommendations for each.

01

Adjust the price

Bring the asking price closer to what 55+ buyers are actually paying, so the home shows up in more searches and stands out once it does.

02

Refresh the photos

Once your things are moved out, a clean, cleared-out home and fresh shots of the trees and fenced yard would show buyers what makes this lot special.

03

Offer a carpet & paint allowance

A credit toward the cosmetic updates buyers already expect to make can make the home feel move-in ready on paper.

04

Lead with the monthly savings

Once I confirm the exact lot rent with the park owner, we can advertise the monthly number and show buyers what it saves them over other parks.
